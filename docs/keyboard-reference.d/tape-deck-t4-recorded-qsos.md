Flat key, new:

- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F11** — Open Recorded QSOs, the computer recordings (Global, works with no radio)

JJ key, actions (Alt plus a letter), new:

- **Alt+F** — Open Recorded QSOs — F for files. This spends the Alt+F that #515's unruled "Alt means speak it" proposal used as its example.

Inside the Recorded QSOs window, new: Up and Down choose, Home and End go to the newest and oldest, Enter plays or stops, Left and Right step by the step size, [ and ] mark the trim start and end, P pauses and resumes, Delete moves to the Recycle Bin, Escape stops a preview and then closes.
