JJ Flexible Home, Shift+S (bench ruling 2026-10-25):

- In Modern tuning, Shift+S speaks the coarse and fine step sizes from every Home field. Until now only the Frequency field had it, although the Tuning menu's Speak Current Step showed it as if it worked anywhere.
- In Classic tuning, Shift+S gives the usual "Shift S does nothing on" answer for the field you are on.

Already applied in docs/help/md/keyboard-reference.md (the universal Home keys, and the Modern Frequency field) and docs/help/md/jj-flexible-home.md (Universal Keys). No global or leader binding was added, removed or remapped.
