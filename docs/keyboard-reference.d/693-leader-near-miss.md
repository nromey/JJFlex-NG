JJ key layer, after a near miss (#693):

- The layer stays armed after a near miss for H, the slash key, Escape and the chord the near miss named. Pressing that chord runs it without Ctrl+J again. Any other key lets go, as before.
- From another program, through the system-wide JJ key, the hook claims the named chord the same way.
- A layer waiting inside the window (for its second key, or after an unknown key or a near miss) lets go when you switch to another program. A near miss made in the window is not carried to the other program.

Already applied in docs/help/md/keyboard-reference.md (the JJ key section, "Press a key the layer doesn't know") and docs/help/md/leader-key.md ("When the JJ layer doesn't know the key you pressed"). No binding was added, removed or remapped.
