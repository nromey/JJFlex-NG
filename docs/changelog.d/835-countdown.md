Check your audio now counts you in before it transmits, setting the check up while it counts, and the Fix tool's countdown follows the same settings. You can turn it off, and choose its length, pitch, sound and pip length, in Settings on the Audio tab under Countdown, with a Play sample button to hear your choice first.

During the count, pressing Control on its own skips the rest of it and transmits straight away; Escape still stops the check without transmitting.
