- **The Flex Control knob no longer acts on turns it saved up while nobody was listening** — when the knob gets bumped or twirled while JJ Flexible isn't connected to it, the knob saves those turns and hands them all over the moment it connects, and the radio used to jump by however many steps had piled up. Now, for about a third of a second after it connects, JJ Flexible ignores the knob's turns and presses, which throws away what it saved up. If you're turning the knob in that moment, that turning is ignored too. Every turn and press after that arrives.
