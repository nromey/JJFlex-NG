- **The Flex Control knob no longer acts on turns it saved up while nobody was listening** — when the knob gets bumped or twirled while JJ Flexible isn't connected to it, the knob saves those turns and hands them all over the moment it connects, and the radio used to jump by however many steps had piled up. Now JJ Flexible ignores the knob for the brief moment right after it connects, usually a few hundredths of a second and never more than about a third of a second, so that pile is thrown away. After that, every turn and every press you make arrives. The one catch: if you happen to be turning the knob in that very first instant, that little bit of turning is ignored too.
