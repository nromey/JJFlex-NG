- **The Flex Control knob no longer acts on a pile of turns nobody made** — when the knob gets bumped or twirled while JJ Flexible isn't listening to it, the knob saves those turns up and hands them all over the moment it connects, and the radio used to jump by however many steps had piled up. The same pile-up could land if the app was busy for a second or two. Now, when three or more turns arrive faster than a hand could possibly make them, they're all thrown away. One or two saved steps can still come through, because nothing can tell those from you turning the knob right then. Your own turning, brisk turning included, still comes through, every step.
