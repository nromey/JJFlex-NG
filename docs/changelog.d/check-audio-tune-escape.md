- Check your audio and the tune carrier get along now. With tune on, Escape in Check your audio turns tune off, with the usual tone and SWR reading, and the window stays open; a second Escape closes it. It no longer blames a hardware PTT line or VOX for a carrier the app put up itself. Your tune key (Ctrl+Shift+T unless you've changed it) works inside the window too: it always turns tune off, and it won't start tune while a check is running.
