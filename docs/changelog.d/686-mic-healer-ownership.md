- **On a radio that isn't yours, the app no longer flips the owner's microphone input back to PC behind their back** — while you're sending computer audio, the app keeps an eye on whether the radio is still listening to that audio, and if the radio's mic input moved somewhere else, it put it straight back to PC. On your own radio that's still exactly what happens. On a friend's radio it was the wrong call: if your friend picked up the hand mic on their own rig, the app quietly undid their choice. Now, on a radio you've marked as someone else's, or one you haven't answered the "Whose radio is this" question for yet, it leaves the change alone and tells you that the radio's mic input changed, so the radio won't hear you. That change can come from someone at the radio, from another program connected to it, or from a profile being loaded, and the app doesn't guess which.
- **Switch Mic Input Back to PC is new on the Radio menu** — that warning points you at it. Pressing it puts the radio's mic input back on PC, because you asked, and you hear "Mic input switched back to PC" only once the radio says it really did. If the radio doesn't confirm the switch, you're told that, along with the last input it reported, and the app doesn't pretend to know more. When you stop computer audio afterwards, it still puts the owner's setting back, in case the radio switched without saying so. If there's nothing to switch, because computer audio isn't running or the input is already on PC, it tells you that instead, and if you've set "change nothing" on that radio, that wins. The new sentences are first drafts and may change.
- **Starting computer audio switches the mic input to PC on any radio, and stopping it puts it back** — you can't transmit from your computer any other way, so it switches whoever owns the radio. When you stop, the old setting goes back, but only if the input is still on PC the way the app left it. If someone moved it in between, whether that was you, the radio's owner at the radio, or another program on it, their choice stays. If you switched it back to PC from the Radio menu along the way, stopping puts back what the owner had chosen just before that.
- **If computer audio fails to start, the radio's mic input goes back where it was** — starting computer audio switches the mic input to PC first and starts your sound card second, and when the sound card didn't start, the radio could be left on PC with no computer audio running at all. Now the old setting is put back straight away, on the same terms as a normal stop. A setting saved from one radio is never put back on a different radio after a reconnect, and the warning that the mic input was changed speaks up again in each new computer-audio session instead of staying quiet because it had already spoken in an earlier one.
- **That microphone check now works on a computer that's been running for weeks** — on a machine left on for somewhere between about 25 and 50 days, a clock quirk meant the check never ran at all, so nothing would have told you your computer audio wasn't reaching the transmitter. It runs no matter how long the computer has been up.
