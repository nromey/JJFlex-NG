- **On a radio that isn't yours, the app no longer flips the owner's microphone input back to PC** — while you're sending computer audio, the app keeps an eye on whether the radio is still listening to that audio, and if the radio's mic input moved somewhere else, it put it straight back to PC. On your own radio that's still exactly what happens. On a friend's radio it was the wrong call: if your friend picked up the hand mic on their own rig, the app quietly undid their choice. Now, on a radio you've marked as someone else's, or one you haven't answered the "Whose radio is this" question for yet, it leaves the setting alone and tells you that audio from your computer can't reach the transmitter, and why. If the radio really is yours, mark it so under Settings, Per-radio settings, and the old put-it-back behaviour returns. That sentence is a first draft and may change.
- **That microphone check now works on a computer that's been running for weeks** — on a machine left on for somewhere between about 25 and 50 days, a clock quirk meant the check never ran at all, so nothing would have told you your computer audio wasn't reaching the transmitter. It runs no matter how long the computer has been up.
- **Turning computer audio off no longer undoes a mic change made while it was on** — starting computer audio switches the radio's mic input to PC, and stopping it puts back whatever was there before. If someone moved the mic input in between, whether that was you, the radio's owner at the radio, or another program on it, stopping computer audio used to drag it back to the old setting anyway. Now it only puts the old setting back if the input is still on PC, the way the app left it.
