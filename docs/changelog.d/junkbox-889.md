- On a radio that offers RF gain as just two settings, like the FLEX-6300's 0 and 20, Home now gives you an RF preamp switch instead of a number field with only two stops. It says "RF preamp on, 20 dB" or "RF preamp off", and the Radio menu's RF Gain Up and Down say the same. Radios with a real range keep the number field, and its limits now follow what the radio reports, including after a band change.
