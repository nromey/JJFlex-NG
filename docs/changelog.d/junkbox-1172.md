- The US 60 meter channel keys, Alt+Shift+Up and Alt+Shift+Down, now cycle through four channels and a segment stop; the former 5,358.5 kilohertz channel is no longer a channel stop.
- The channel keys now set the dial to the channel centre in CW and 1.5 kilohertz below the centre in upper sideband or Digital USB, instead of always using the literal centre of the channel. Each channel announcement gives the stop's frequency and mode, adjusted for mode. - CW and Digital USB now stay selected when you move to a channel using the channel change keys, instead of both modes switching to the upper sideband by default. Channel navigation changes lower sideband to upper sideband, and Digital LSB or RTTY to Digital USB to match 60 meter band requirements established by the FCC.
- The segment stop has moved from 5,351.5 to 5,357.0 kilohertz and keeps upper sideband, lower sideband, CW, Digital USB, Digital LSB and RTTY. A remembered sideband frequency near either edge of the segment now returns far enough inside for the default transmit filter; this adjustment does not check filters you have changed.
- An older band memory of 5,332.0 kilohertz in upper sideband now returns at 5,330.5 kilohertz when you jump back to 60 meters; the same 1.5 kilohertz correction applies to the other remaining channels. A frequency typed directly is kept as entered.
