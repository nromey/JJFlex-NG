- The 60 meter channels now follow the FCC's current rule, in force since February 2026. There are four channels, not five: the old 5358.5 kilohertz channel is gone, because it now sits inside the new segment from 5351.5 to 5366.5 kilohertz.
- A 60 meter channel stop now tunes where the rule puts your signal. In upper sideband or digital upper sideband it tunes 1.5 kilohertz below the channel centre (5330.5, 5346.5, 5371.5 and 5403.5), so your signal sits inside the channel instead of half outside it. In CW it tunes the channel centre. The stop names the frequency you are actually on.
- CW and digital upper sideband are now kept on the channels instead of being switched to upper sideband. Lower sideband, digital lower sideband and the radio's RTTY mode are switched to upper sideband or digital upper sideband there.
- Voice is now allowed in the segment. Upper and lower sideband, CW, the digital sidebands and RTTY are all kept there; AM, FM and FreeDV are switched to upper sideband. The segment stop is 5357.0 kilohertz, which keeps your whole signal inside the segment in any of those modes.
- If your band memory held a 60 meter upper sideband frequency at a channel centre from an earlier version, the band jump now brings you back 1.5 kilohertz lower, on the right spot. A frequency you type is never moved.
- The segment's power limit is much lower than the channels': 9.15 watts effective radiated power, against 100 on the channels. Nothing in the app limits your power for this yet.
