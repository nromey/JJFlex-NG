- With AGC off you can finally set the gain. The AGC field on Home now follows the AGC mode: while AGC is on it is the AGC threshold, as before, and while AGC is off it becomes "AGC Off Gain", the fixed gain the radio uses in its place. The Radio menu's AGC Threshold Up and Down are now called AGC Level Up and AGC Level Down; they follow the same rule and say which one they changed. Before, turning AGC off left the receiver quiet with no way to bring it back up short of turning AGC on again.
