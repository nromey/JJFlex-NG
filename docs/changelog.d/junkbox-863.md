When a check, a send or push to talk inside a window is about to transmit on a slice other than your active slice, you now hear where, for example "Transmitting on slice B, 14.100", the first time, and again only when the transmit slice, the active slice or the transmit frequency changes. Every time it keys and unkeys there, you hear a three-note tone instead of the usual two. A check or a send waits until the slice has been said, and if you change slices while it waits, it stops rather than transmit somewhere you were not told about; if the sentence cannot even start within ten seconds, it stops then too. You can choose tones only, or nothing, under Settings, PTT, "Transmitting on another slice"; Check your audio always says where it will transmit.
