If the radio's connection drops while you are transmitting, your key now drops with it: you hear the warning tone and a sentence saying the connection dropped and that JJ Flexible has let go of its own transmit key. A voice message, a test tone or a check that was under way stops too, and a push-to-talk or lock key you were still holding will not key the radio again when it comes back until you let go and press it afresh. The same goes for disconnecting, switching radios or closing the app while you are transmitting: the key comes up first.
