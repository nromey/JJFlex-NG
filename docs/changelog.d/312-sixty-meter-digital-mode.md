- **Stepping into the 60 meter digital segment now switches to the proper mode to match 60 meter band guidance** — Alt+Shift+Up and Alt+Shift+Down walk the five 60 meter channels and the digital segment. The channels have always set USB, but stepping from a channel into the digital segment left you on USB, inside a part of the band where voice isn't allowed, and only told you where you'd arrived. Now, if you arrive on a mode that isn't allowed there, the radio is asked to switch to CW and you hear it: "60 meter digital and CW segment, 5.3515 megahertz, digital segment needs CW, switching." If you were already on CW or a digital mode, it's left alone.
- **Typing a 60 meter frequency changes to the proper mode as well** — type a frequency in the digital segment on the frequency field and press Enter while you're on a voice mode, and you'll hear "Frequency set to 5.360 megahertz, digital segment needs CW, switching." Typing one of the five channels switches to USB, the same as jumping to 60 meters and stepping through the channels already do. Anywhere else on the dial, typing a frequency works exactly as before.
- **You no longer hear "mode set" for a mode that wasn't** — this goes for jumping to 60 meters too, which used to say "mode set to" whether or not the mode changed. Now you hear "switching" when the request went to the radio, and if the mode can't be changed right then, you hear that instead, for example "digital segment needs CW, mode not changed while transmitting".
