- **Stepping into the 60 meter digital segment now sets the mode for you** — Alt+Shift+Up and Alt+Shift+Down walk the five 60 meter channels and the digital segment. The channels have always set USB, but stepping from a channel into the digital segment left you on USB, inside a part of the band where voice isn't allowed, and only told you where you'd arrived. Now, if you arrive on a mode that isn't allowed there, the radio is switched to CW and you hear it: "60 meter digital and CW segment, 5.3515 megahertz, mode set to CW for digital segment." If you were already on CW or a digital mode, it's left alone. This is the same thing jumping to 60 meters has done for a while; the step keys just hadn't caught up.
