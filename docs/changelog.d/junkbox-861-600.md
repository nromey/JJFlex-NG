When you disconnect while using your computer's audio, JJ Flexible now stops transmitting first, then puts the radio's microphone input back and the shack speaker back the way it found them before it hangs up, so whoever is in the room hears the radio again, and a speaker that was muted when you connected stays muted. If someone else on the radio has changed either one since, their choice is left alone, and if the radio is still transmitting for any reason, the microphone input is left where it is rather than switched under the carrier. When that happens you now hear it, after the disconnect: the microphone input is still set to PC, so you can change it the next time you connect to that radio.

When the radio is too busy to switch its microphone input to PC as your computer's audio starts, or when you choose Switch Mic Input Back to PC, JJ Flexible keeps trying once a second instead of giving up, and if it is still trying after five seconds you hear "Still switching mic to PC". Choosing a microphone input yourself ends the trying, and your choice stands.
