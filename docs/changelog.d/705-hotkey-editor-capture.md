In the Hotkey Editor, Tab and Shift+Tab now move you on while the editor is waiting for a new key, instead of being held back with a sentence telling you Tab is for moving around. Escape still cancels, and pressing Enter now tells you plainly that Enter can't be assigned. The tab order makes more sense too: from the list of commands, Shift+Tab takes you back to Arrange by and Category, and Tab takes you straight to Change Key, rather than round the long way.
