- **Your saved station now counts as restored when it actually is** — since late September, every time you connected to your own radio and your saved station came back, the app still said "Station restore not confirmed" and quietly skipped the rest of its connect-time setup: your transmit and microphone profiles, TNF, mic input, VOX off, break-in off, TX1 and your keyer settings. The restore itself was fine; the app simply had no way to tell it had finished, and said so on every connect. It now watches for what the radio does when a profile load completes (your slices come back, then the radio re-sends its profile list) and treats that as done. Connect to your own radio with nobody else on it, your station comes back, nothing is said about it, and the transmit and microphone setup runs the way it did before September. Seen on an 8600 over the local network and over SmartLink; if you are on a 6300 and still hear the sentence on every connect, that is worth a report.
- **The "not confirmed" sentence says what it means** — on the connects where the app genuinely cannot tell whether your station came back, it used to say "nothing added on top", which meant nothing to anyone but its author. It now says the restore was not confirmed, so transmit and microphone setup was skipped. First draft; the wording may change.
