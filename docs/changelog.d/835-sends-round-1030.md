In Recorded QSOs, Enter now sends the recording you have selected while you hold push to talk or the transmit lock, the same as Play from computer does, so a recording goes out the same way whichever key you use.

When you pause a recording playing on your speakers, VOX now comes back on, so you can talk about what you just heard. Carrying on pauses VOX again before the recording sounds, so your microphone still can't key the radio on your speakers.

A recording sent on the transmit lock, a bulletin for example, is no longer cut off at the lock's three minutes: the ten-minute pause looks after it instead. And when you take a send over with push to talk, the transmit monitor now stays the way it was until you let go.
