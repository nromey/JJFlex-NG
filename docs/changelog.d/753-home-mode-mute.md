Your Home slice row now has a Mode field: arrow to it to hear the slice's mode, and press Space or Up for the next mode or Down for the previous one. The Mute field is on the row in Modern tuning too, so you can hear whether a slice is muted just by arriving on it, instead of toggling mute to find out and toggling it back. Right after it comes the Volume field, in Modern tuning as well as Classic now: arrow to it to hear the slice's volume, and press Up or Down to change it.
