- Filter widths above 1 kilohertz now say exactly what they are, so stepping a filter 50 hertz at a time never repeats a width or reads one 50 hertz off. At Terse verbosity you hear the short form, like "Filter 100 to 2850, 2.75 k"; at Chatty you hear the whole number with a word to say what it is, like "Filter 100 to 2850, 2750 hertz wide". The width readouts in the filter preset list and the Audio Workshop show the exact value too.
