- **Saving or deleting a profile on a radio that isn't yours is now refused** — "Save Station Setup to Radio" writes the radio's global profile, which is the one everybody who connects to that radio loads, and the Profiles dialog's Save and Delete buttons change the same shared list. Until now, if you were on a friend's radio by yourself and chose any of them, it went through, and your friend got your layout, or lost a profile, the next time they connected. The app now checks whose radio it is first. If you've marked the radio as someone else's, or you've never answered the question, it tells you so and changes nothing on the radio.
- **If the app doesn't know yet whether a radio is yours, it says where to tell it** — Settings, under Radios, "Whose radio is this". That matters because the question can go unanswered for a long time without you knowing it was ever asked, and until you answer it, saving and deleting are refused on your own radio too. These sentences are first drafts and may change.
- **"Saved" now means the save actually went out** — if the command to the radio never left the queue, you hear that the profile or station was not saved, and the app keeps treating your changes as unsaved, instead of announcing a save that didn't happen. Before, a refusal could also be followed by a cheerful "saved" for a save that never went anywhere; it isn't any more.
