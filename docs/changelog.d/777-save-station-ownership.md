- **Saving, creating or deleting a profile on a radio that isn't yours is now refused** — "Save Station Setup to Radio" writes the radio's global profile, which is the one everybody who connects to that radio loads, and the Profiles dialog's Save, Select and Delete buttons change the same shared lists, for global, transmit and microphone profiles alike. Until now, if you were on a friend's radio by yourself and chose any of them, it went through, and your friend got your layout, or a new profile, or lost one, the next time they connected. The app now checks whose radio it is first. If you've marked the radio as someone else's, or you've never answered the question, it tells you so, names what it didn't do, and changes nothing on the radio. Selecting a transmit or microphone profile the radio already has still works; it's creating a new one on someone else's radio that is refused.
- **If the app doesn't know yet whether a radio is yours, it says where to tell it** — Settings, under Per-radio settings, "Whose radio is this". That matters because the question can go unanswered for a long time without you knowing it was ever asked, and until you answer it, saving and deleting are refused on your own radio too.
- **"Saved" now means the save command was handed to a connected radio** — if the command to the radio never left the queue, or the connection had already dropped when it was about to go, you hear that the profile or station was not saved, and the app keeps treating your changes as unsaved, instead of announcing a save that didn't happen. Before, a refusal could also be followed by a cheerful "saved" for a save that never went anywhere; it isn't any more. The not-saved sentences are first drafts and may change.
