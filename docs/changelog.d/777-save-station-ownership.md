- **Saving the station to a radio that isn't yours is now refused** — "Save Station Setup to Radio" writes the radio's global profile, which is the one everybody who connects to that radio loads. Until now, if you were on a friend's radio by yourself and chose it, it went through, and your friend got your layout the next time they connected. The app now checks whose radio it is first. If you've marked the radio as someone else's, or you've never answered the question, it tells you so and saves nothing. The same check now sits under the Profiles dialog's Save button, which had none at all. And if a save is refused, the dialog no longer follows the refusal with a cheerful "saved" for a save that didn't happen.
- **If the app doesn't know yet whether a radio is yours, it says where to tell it** — Settings, under Radios, "Whose radio is this". That matters because the question can go unanswered for a long time without you knowing it was ever asked, and until you answer it the save is refused on your own radio too. These sentences are first drafts and may change.
