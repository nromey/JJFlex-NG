- The US 60 meter rules are now enforced when you transmit, and the setting is on by default for a US operator: a transmission on 60 meters is refused unless it is on one of the four channels at the exact frequency for your mode, or inside the segment, with your whole signal inside and no wider than 2.8 kilohertz. The refusal tells you what was wrong. The License tab's checkbox, now called Enforce 60 meter transmit rules, turns this off, separately from the TX band lockout. Power on 60 meters is still not limited, and tune and the antenna tuner are not checked.
