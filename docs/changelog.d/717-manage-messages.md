Manage Messages now says so when you have no messages yet, instead of leaving you on a silent list. Its way out is a Close button rather than one labelled Cancel, because every add, update and delete is saved the moment you make it; Escape still closes it. Deleting your last message leaves you in the dialog on the empty list, ready to add another, rather than closing it out from under you. And landing on the list no longer reads you a paragraph of instructions every time; press Ctrl+F1 there when you want them.
