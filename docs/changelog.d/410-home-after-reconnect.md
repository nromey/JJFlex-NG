- **A quick reconnect now brings Home all the way back** — if the radio dropped and came back within three minutes, the field panel used to stay missing: you'd Tab from the frequency and the fields you expected simply weren't there, and reconnecting again didn't help, because reconnecting was exactly the thing that left them out. Home now puts the field panel back the same way it does after a longer outage, so a short drop costs you nothing but the drop. The panadapter comes back on its own as soon as the radio starts sending it. And if you had the meters panel open before the drop, it's open again afterwards, after a short drop or a long one; until now it always came back closed.
