- **A quick reconnect now brings Home all the way back** — if the radio's connection dropped and came back within three minutes, the field panel used to stay missing: you'd Tab from the frequency and the fields you expected simply weren't there. Home now puts the field panel back the same way it does after a longer outage, so a short disconnection costs you nothing but the drop. The panadapter also comes back on its own as soon as the radio starts sending data.
