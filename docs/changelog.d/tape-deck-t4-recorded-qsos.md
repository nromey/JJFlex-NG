- Recorded QSOs (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F11, or the JJ key then Alt+F) lists your computer recordings, so you can pick the one Play from computer plays, listen to it, trim it, save it as MP3, Opus, WAV, FLAC or M4A, and send old ones to the Recycle Bin.
