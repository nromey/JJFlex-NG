Period and comma make and release slices from anywhere on your Home slice row now, not just from the Slice field: press period on the S Meter and you get a new slice, press comma and the current one goes. On the Frequency field they know what you're doing. Start typing a frequency and the period is your decimal point, so 14.2 and Enter still takes you to 14.200, and a comma is a thousands separator, so 14,225.000 takes you to 14.225; press period before any digit and it makes a slice instead. If you want a frequency under one megahertz, type the zero first.
