- Play takes is a new window that lists every take of your audio on this computer, newest first, whatever radio or method made it. You can play a take, compare it with another one you choose, read its details, add a note, save a copy as WAV or FLAC, or delete it. Ctrl+J then Alt+T opens it, and Check your audio has a Play takes button.
- A comparison now names more of what changed between two takes: mic gain, the compander, the speech processor and the transmit equalizer. When more than one setting changed, it says so.
- Check your audio's three methods that make a take now have clearer names: Radio recording, Heard on the air, and Looped back.
- Check your audio can now save what the radio plays back as a take. Tick "Save each playback as a take" with Radio recording or Looped back, and each playback lands in Play takes, numbered in a test you can rename and come back to. While it plays, your other slices go quiet so only your take is recorded, and they come back on by themselves when it ends, however it ends. It is off until you turn it on, and remembered for each radio.
- When you change radio, mic profile, mic input or computer microphone between takes, Check your audio asks whether to start a new test or add to the one you are in. Say "add to this test" once and it stops asking for that test.
- Play last take plays your saved take when there is one.
- When a reference recording finishes, the transmission now ends with it. In Check your audio the check stops just as if you had pressed Stop, and with Radio recording your take plays straight away. If you keyed with JJ Flexible's push to talk or the lock instead, it lets go of the key for you. Your microphone no longer goes out on the air after the reference: silence goes out until the radio is back on receive.
- If you are still holding your radio's own push to talk or footswitch when a reference recording finishes, JJ Flexible keeps sending silence instead of your microphone and tells you to release it, so nothing you say by accident goes out.
- A message or recording send now tells you it can't go while a reference recording is armed in Check your audio, and the reference won't arm while a send is going out, so one never replaces the other halfway through.
- Pressing Play in Check your audio while something is still transmitting now tells you what to wait for: the check to finish, or the radio to stop. It used to tell you to stop the check, even when the check wasn't what was keeping the radio on the air.
- When JJ Flexible lets go of its push to talk or lock and the radio keeps transmitting anyway, because a hardware PTT line, a stuck microphone or VOX is still holding it, you now hear that the radio is still transmitting instead of "Receiving", without having to press Stop or Escape a second time. In Check your audio it says the check is complete but the radio is still transmitting. An ordinary release still says "Receiving", once the radio confirms it.
- The reference recording that ships with JJ Flexible now reads a little faster, every word kept, so it runs 1 minute 53 seconds instead of 2 minutes 8. That fits inside the radio's two-minute recorder, so a Radio recording check that sends it ends with the reference and plays your take back, instead of stopping at the recorder's limit with the last few seconds missing.
