# Audio Levels — the two dialogs

There are two completely different kinds of "volume" in a remote rig, and JJ Flexible keeps them deliberately apart: the levels on *this computer's* side of the wire, and the levels on the *radio's own jacks*. The Audio menu holds one door for each.

## PC Audio Levels

Audio menu, **PC Audio Levels (this computer)**. This is the dialog for how the radio sounds *through your computer* — the side of the wire a remote operator actually hears.

- **PC Output Volume** — how loud radio audio plays through your PC, in dB of boost, 0 to 24. This is the volume remote operators have always wanted; 12 dB is the loudness you were living with before it became adjustable. Remembered between sessions.
- **Mic Level** — your transmit audio level. Applies to PC audio too.

## On-Radio Levels

Audio menu, **On-Radio Levels (the radio's own jacks)**. Everything here moves hardware on the radio itself — useful when you or someone else is in the same room as the rig, silent over a PC audio stream from across town. That's exactly why "On-Radio" is in every name.

- **On-Radio Headphone Volume** and **On-Radio Line Out Volume**.
- **Mute Headphone Jack**, **Mute Line Out**, and **Mute Front Speaker**.

## How the dialogs work

Both dialogs behave the same way:

- Arrow to a level and ride it with Up and Down — the dialog stays open, so five nudges are five keystrokes, not five trips through the menu. Shift + Up / Down moves by one for fine adjustment, Page Up / Page Down jumps by ten, Home and End go to the ends of the range.
- Every change speaks its new value and applies to the radio immediately. There's no OK button because there's nothing to confirm — the radio is already doing what you asked.
- Escape closes the dialog.
- If a level changes from somewhere else while a dialog is open — the Home expander, volume mode, another client on your radio — the field follows along quietly.

## The other two routes

The dialogs are the discoverable, see-everything-at-once route. The same levels also live:

- In the **Audio expander on JJ Flexible Home** — `Ctrl+Shift+U`, then arrow. In-context, no window at all. This is also where your slice's Volume and Pan live: those are per-slice controls rather than levels on either side of the wire, so they stay with the slice.
- On **volume mode** — `Ctrl+J`, then `V`, then a target letter, then arrows. The fastest route once it's muscle memory; see the keyboard reference under "Volume mode."

Use whichever fits your style. They all move the same levels.
