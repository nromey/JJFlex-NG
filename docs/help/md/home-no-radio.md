# Home Without a Radio

When JJ Flexible starts and no radio is connected — the radio's powered down, you're away from the shack, or you just closed the picker to look around — Home becomes a short, honest page instead of a wall of controls that couldn't do anything. The window title says so too: it ends with "no radio connected", so the very first thing your screen reader announces tells you where you stand.

The rule behind this page is simple: everything on it genuinely works without a radio. Nothing here will pretend to change a rig that isn't listening.

## What's on the page

Five buttons, in tab order:

- **Connect a radio** — opens the radio picker to look for radios on your network and your SmartLink account. If nothing is found, you land right back here, no harm done.
- **Settings** — every JJ Flexible setting, including per-radio setup for radios you are not connected to right now. A change that needs a radio waits patiently and applies at your next connection.
- **Audio Workshop** — the microphone check and this computer's audio setup work fully with no radio. The Workshop's radio-side controls (mic gain, compander, and friends) quietly disable themselves until a radio arrives, so nothing in there can claim a change it didn't make.
- **Help** — opens this help file.
- **Exit** — closes JJ Flexible.

## Getting the full Home back

Connect a radio. The moment a connection lands, this page steps aside and the full JJ Flexible Home — frequency, slices, S-meter, all of it — takes its place. See the JJ Flexible Home help page for the tour.

One detail worth knowing: this page appears at startup only. If a radio you were using disconnects mid-session, you stay on the full Home — the app announces the disconnect and offers the picker, rather than yanking the room out from under you.
