# Receive chain rules for JJ Flexible.
#
# Why a radio is making no sound, walked in the order the faults actually bite.
# Same file format, same analyzer and same override path as tx-chain-rules.txt:
# read that file's header for how to write a rule. An operator's own copy at
# %AppData%\JJFlexRadio\rx-chain-rules.txt replaces this one entirely.
#
#
# WHY THIS FILE EXISTS, added 2026-08-25.
#
# The receive check used to be thirty-five lines of C# inside
# FlexBase.SilentRadioAdvisory: an if-ladder of seven conditions, first match
# wins, returning ONE sentence and nothing else. No stage names, no evidence
# block, no way to say what it could NOT check, and not a word of it
# correctable without shipping a new binary.
#
# Sitting beside it was the transmit walk: thirteen stages, twenty-one rules,
# in text an operator can edit. Two implementations of the same idea, and only
# one of them could be improved by anybody who was not building the app.
#
# The ladder's ORDER was right and is preserved exactly, because it encodes
# something real: a mute is worse news than a low level, and both are worse
# news than a routing choice. Say the most disabling thing first.

#
# STAGE 5 IS A DIFFERENT KIND OF CHECK, added 2026-08-28 (#350).
#
# Stages 0 to 4 read SETTINGS: a slice mute and volume, output mutes and
# levels, and a routing switch. Every one of them is something this application
# asked the radio to be, so the whole report could be verifiably correct while
# no audio had ever reached the computer. It said the plumbing was configured;
# it never said water came out.
#
# Stage 5 reads what ARRIVED — bytes the radio sent that crossed the network
# and were counted on this side. That is the half a manufacturer's support desk
# asks about first, and the only half that means anything to a reader who does
# not trust our software.
#
# Every rule in it is gated on pc-audio, and that gate is not optional. The
# network audio stream only carries sound to this computer when radio audio
# through this computer is switched on; an operator listening on the radio's
# own speaker has no such stream, measures zero, and has nothing wrong with
# their station. An ungated rule here would accuse the commonest setup there is.

#
# A NOTE ON NAMING KEYS IN A FIX, added 2026-08-29 (#367).
#
# Three remedies here named Control plus J, then A, "from anywhere in the
# application" — true of the main window and of ordinary dialogs, and FALSE on
# the Fixer's own page, which is a WebView2 island that keeps its own
# keystrokes (FixerDialog says exactly that about Escape, and builds a bridge
# for it). Joining the receive check's two doors put this text on that page.
#
# The key is still worth naming; the claim about where it reaches is not. The
# remedies name the main window and stop there.

ruleset: Receive audio check
version: 3
symptom: you still cannot hear the radio


# ── Stage 0 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

stage: 0 the connection to your radio
about: A Flex makes no audio at all until a client connects to it.

rule: rx-no-radio
in-stage: 0
broken-when: radio-connected is no
verdict: No radio is connected. A Flex makes no sound at all until a client connects to it, and that includes its own headphone jack on the front panel. This catches nearly everybody once.
fix: Connect to your radio, then run this test again.


# ── Stage 1 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# THE SLICE, added 2026-08-29 (#367), and it goes here because the signal path
# does: a slice's own mute and volume sit upstream of every output the radio
# has AND of the network stream, so a muted slice is silent in all of them at
# once. That makes it the most disabling thing in this file after having no
# radio at all, and this file says the most disabling thing first.
#
# It existed only as a sentence until now. The Audio Workshop's all-clear text
# ended "if the radio is still silent, check the slice volume and that a slice
# is not muted" — real advice, hand-written, with no rule, no fact and no
# evidence line behind it. Joining the receive check's two doors retired that
# string, and a rung living only in retired prose is a rung that is gone.
#
# Every rule here is gated on active-slice at RULE level rather than by a
# stage-level needs:, because the fact is a flag and a stage gated on a flag
# currently reports "not part of your receive path, because a slice is
# receiving is no" (#371). The stage's nothing-to-check line says it properly.

stage: 1 the slice you are listening to
about: A slice's own mute and volume, which sit ahead of every output the radio has and of the audio sent to this computer.
nothing-to-check: no slice is receiving, so there is no slice mute or slice volume to read. Start a slice, then run this test again.

rule: rx-slice-muted
in-stage: 1
needs: active-slice is yes
broken-when: slice-muted is yes
verdict: The slice you are listening to is muted. Muting a slice silences it everywhere at once — headphones, line out, the front speaker, and the audio sent to this computer — so nothing further down will make a sound while it stays muted.
fix: Unmute the slice. On Home, M mutes and unmutes the slice you are on, and there is a Mute box in the Audio panel.
evidence: slice-muted
evidence: active-slice

rule: rx-slice-level-zero
in-stage: 1
needs: active-slice is yes
broken-when: slice-level at most 0
verdict: The slice you are listening to has its volume at zero. That is a different control from the radio's output levels and it comes before them, so nothing after it can make a sound.
fix: Raise Volume in the Audio panel on Home for the slice you are on.
evidence: slice-level
evidence: active-slice

rule: rx-slice-level-very-low
in-stage: 1
needs: active-slice is yes
broken-when: slice-level at most 5
verdict: The slice you are listening to has its volume very low, at {slice-level} out of 100. There may well be sound there, but not enough of it to hear over a room.
fix: Raise Volume in the Audio panel on Home for the slice you are on.
evidence: slice-level
evidence: active-slice


# ── Stage 2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Mutes before levels, and all-three before any one. An operator who is told
# "the headphone output is muted" and fixes it, only to find the line out was
# muted too, has been sent round twice by a check that knew both answers.

stage: 2 the radio's outputs
about: Whether the radio's own outputs are muted.

rule: rx-everything-muted
in-stage: 2
broken-when: headphone-muted is yes
broken-when: lineout-muted is yes
broken-when: front-speaker-muted is yes
verdict: All three of the radio's outputs are currently muted — headphones, line out and the front speaker. Nothing you do anywhere else will produce a sound until at least one of them is unmuted.
fix: In Settings, Audio, unmute whichever output you actually listen through. All three are there.
evidence: headphone-muted
evidence: lineout-muted
evidence: front-speaker-muted

rule: rx-headphone-and-lineout-muted
in-stage: 2
broken-when: headphone-muted is yes
broken-when: lineout-muted is yes
verdict: The radio's headphone and line out outputs are both currently muted. The front speaker is not, so if you have one you may still be hearing it.
fix: In Settings, Audio, unmute whichever of the two you listen through.
evidence: headphone-muted
evidence: lineout-muted

rule: rx-headphone-muted
in-stage: 2
broken-when: headphone-muted is yes
verdict: The radio's headphone output is currently muted.
fix: In Settings, Audio, unmute the headphone output.
evidence: headphone-muted

rule: rx-lineout-muted
in-stage: 2
broken-when: lineout-muted is yes
verdict: The radio's line out output is currently muted.
fix: In Settings, Audio, unmute the line out output.
evidence: lineout-muted


# ── Stage 3 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

stage: 3 the radio's output levels
about: How loud the radio is set to be, which on most Flex models is the only volume control there is.

rule: rx-both-levels-zero
in-stage: 3
broken-when: headphone-level at most 0
broken-when: lineout-level at most 0
verdict: Both of the radio's output levels are currently at zero. On a Flex with no front panel knob these are the only volume controls there are, so nothing will make a sound until one of them comes up.
fix: In Settings, Audio, raise the level for whichever output you listen through.
evidence: headphone-level
evidence: lineout-level

rule: rx-headphone-level-zero
in-stage: 3
broken-when: headphone-level at most 0
verdict: The radio's headphone level is currently at zero.
fix: Raise the headphone level in Settings, Audio.
evidence: headphone-level

rule: rx-lineout-level-zero
in-stage: 3
broken-when: lineout-level at most 0
verdict: The radio's line out level is currently at zero.
fix: Raise the line out level in Settings, Audio.
evidence: lineout-level

rule: rx-both-levels-very-low
in-stage: 3
broken-when: headphone-level at most 5
broken-when: lineout-level at most 5
verdict: Both of the radio's output levels are currently very low. There may well be sound there, but not enough of it to hear over a room.
fix: In Settings, Audio, raise the level for whichever output you listen through.
evidence: headphone-level
evidence: lineout-level


# ── Stage 4 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Last, because it is a routing CHOICE rather than a fault — except on a
# remote connection, where it is the whole ball game: there is no other path
# for the sound to take.

stage: 4 how the audio reaches you
about: Whether radio audio is being carried to this computer, which on a remote connection is the only way to hear it.
nothing-to-check: you are not connected remotely, so how the audio travels is a choice rather than a fault: the radio's own outputs are in the room with you whichever way it is set.

rule: rx-pc-audio-off-on-remote
in-stage: 4
needs: remote-radio is yes
broken-when: pc-audio is no
verdict: PC audio is currently switched off and you are connected remotely, so there is no path at all for the sound to reach you. The radio's own headphone jack is at the radio, wherever that is.
fix: Turn PC audio on. Control plus J, then A, does it from the main window, and the setting is also in Settings, Audio.
evidence: pc-audio
evidence: remote-radio


# ── Stage 5 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# The first stage in this file that measures rather than reads back. See the
# note at the top for why it is gated on pc-audio throughout, and why "nothing
# arrived" is reported as three different findings rather than one.
#
# Order, worst news first: nothing at all is arriving, then the radio is
# clearly alive and only the audio is missing, then the general case.
#
# HOW THE CONSISTENCY COUNT IS TAKEN, added 2026-08-28 (#368). The sampler
# starts on connect and audio starts streaming a few seconds later, so a first
# run after any connect holds warm-up zeros at the front of the window. Those
# mean nothing, and counted raw they made a healthy radio read "11 of 16" on
# the measurement's very first field run. So rx-audio-readings counts from the
# first reading that carried audio, and zeros AFTER that point — the holes,
# which are the pattern a weak or congested network makes — are counted
# separately in rx-audio-gaps. When no reading carried audio at all, the two
# cannot be told apart, so rx-audio-readings reports the raw window and
# rx-audio-gaps reports nothing rather than a guess. A rule may read
# rx-audio-gaps (it is a number, "N of readings-since-audio-began"), but no
# shipped rule fires on it yet: how many holes should count as a finding is a
# judgement nobody has made, and the report already states and interprets the
# holes in its measurement sentence either way.

stage: 5 audio arriving from the radio
about: Whether audio is actually reaching this computer, measured from what the radio has been sending over the last half minute rather than from any setting.
nothing-to-check: radio audio through this computer is switched off, so the radio is not sending any audio here and none is expected. What you hear comes out of the radio itself.

rule: rx-nothing-arriving
in-stage: 5
needs: pc-audio is yes
broken-when: rx-audio-kbps at most 0
broken-when: rx-total-kbps at most 0
verdict: Radio audio through this computer is switched on, but nothing at all has been arriving from the radio: over the readings just taken we measured no audio and no other data either. That points at the link between this computer and the radio rather than at any audio setting.
fix: The connection may have dropped without saying so. Disconnect and connect again, which reopens the control link and the audio stream together. If you are connected remotely, check the internet link at both ends first.
evidence: rx-audio-kbps
evidence: rx-audio-readings
evidence: rx-total-kbps
evidence: rx-meter-kbps
evidence: pc-audio
evidence: remote-radio

rule: rx-meters-but-no-audio
in-stage: 5
needs: pc-audio is yes
broken-when: rx-audio-kbps at most 0
broken-when: rx-meter-kbps at least 1
verdict: The radio is talking to this computer — its meter readings are arriving at {rx-meter-kbps} — but no audio is coming with them. Over the readings just taken, audio was present in {rx-audio-readings} readings. The link is alive; it is the audio on it that is missing.
fix: Turn radio audio through this computer off and then on again, which asks the radio for a fresh audio stream. Control plus J, then A, does that from the main window. If it stays silent, disconnect and connect again.
evidence: rx-audio-kbps
evidence: rx-audio-readings
evidence: rx-meter-kbps
evidence: rx-total-kbps
evidence: pc-audio
evidence: remote-radio

rule: rx-data-but-no-audio
in-stage: 5
needs: pc-audio is yes
broken-when: rx-audio-kbps at most 0
broken-when: rx-total-kbps at least 1
verdict: Radio audio through this computer is switched on and data is arriving from the radio at {rx-total-kbps}, but none of it was audio: over the readings just taken, audio was present in {rx-audio-readings} readings.
fix: Ask the radio for a fresh audio stream by turning radio audio through this computer off and then on again — Control plus J, then A. If nothing changes, disconnect and connect again.
evidence: rx-audio-kbps
evidence: rx-audio-readings
evidence: rx-total-kbps
evidence: rx-meter-kbps
evidence: pc-audio
evidence: remote-radio
