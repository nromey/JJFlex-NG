WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE LAST BUILD

The big one is Check your audio, a window of its own for hearing how
you sound on the air before anyone else does. The ones you will
notice:

- Ctrl+J, then Alt+C opens Check your audio. Inside it, Ctrl+Enter
  starts a check. It can record a take off the air and play it back to
  you, and it puts back every radio setting it changed when the check
  ends, even if the radio disconnects partway through.
- Play from computer and Play active slice now send to the air when
  you are transmitting. Ctrl+J, Ctrl+K turns on a box that lets a send
  key the radio by itself while VOX is on. The same box is in Home's
  Transmission group and in Settings, PTT.
- A send longer than three minutes asks first, and tells you how to
  pause it so you can identify.
- If you pause a recording and then press Play with sending turned
  on, it goes to the air from where you paused it. Saying No to the
  long-send question leaves it paused.
- A countdown of tones can play before anything keys. Settings,
  Audio has its length, pitch and an off switch.
- Settings, Audio has an "Alert sound delay". Lower it if alert
  sounds seem to lag behind what they announce.
- The Audio menu has DSP and Meter Tones. The disconnect question has
  a box to stop asking. Warning sounds still play with alert sounds
  turned off, and Logging mode offers a No Log File choice.
- JAWS reads the value of the number fields now.

KNOWN ISSUES

- Just after you turn VOX on, the radio can key itself for about a
  third of a second. It looks like the radio doing it, not the app; I'm
  still looking into it.
- A hand mic or footswitch on the radio's own PTT jack keys the radio
  with the radio's microphone, so your computer microphone is silent
  on the air, and nothing tells you yet. A fix is planned.
- The Fix tool's tone ladder can skip itself, saying the radio did not
  report its mode. With VOX on, its transmit stage can say "passed"
  when it did not transmit at all.
- Shift+Space doesn't lock the transmitter inside dialogs yet. Use
  Alt+Shift+Space there.
