Hello Don — there is one thing I would really like you to do with this build,
and it is new.

TOOLS, THEN FIX, THEN TRANSMIT PROBLEMS

That is JJ Flexible Fix. It walks your transmit chain one stage at a time and
tells you where it dies, instead of leaving you to guess. Nobody has used it on
a real radio yet — you would be the first, and yours is exactly the setup I
most need it tried on: a 6300, over SmartLink, at a real station rather than on
my bench three feet from the rig.

Please just open it and go through it. That is the whole ask.

WHAT TO EXPECT

It asks first what your antenna socket is connected to. Nothing transmits until
you answer that, so if you are not sure, leave it — the checks that transmit
will wait and everything else still runs.

Then five stages, numbered 0 to 4. Stage 0 reads what your computer's audio is
actually doing. Stage 1 listens to your microphone with the radio out of it
entirely. Neither of those keys the radio. Stage 2 onward does transmit, and
each one says so before you run it.

At the bottom there is a report covering the whole run, and a Copy button that
puts an email-ready version on the clipboard.

WHAT I WANT BACK FROM YOU

Not a bug list. Just tell me how it went, in your own words:

- Did it make sense? Could you tell what each stage was asking you?
- Did anything read oddly, or sound like a computer wrote it?
- Did the buttons do what you expected when you pressed them? Genuinely
  unknown, this one — the checks I can run prove a button reaches the
  application, which is not the same as proving it does the right thing to
  your rig.
- Did it find anything, and was the answer useful or just noise?
- Anywhere you got stuck, lost, or had to guess what to do next.

If it is confusing, that is a fault in the tool and not in you. Confusing is
the most useful thing you can report.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD, no action needed

The test tone no longer wobbles on a long transmit. Your keyboard shortcuts
are repaired automatically on first launch if they were written before last
week — if any key does something you did not expect, tell me immediately,
because a wrong key is worse than a missing one. The Audio Workshop's transmit
page is now three shorter pages, and the two meter pages are one.

KNOWN, so you do not waste time reporting it
