WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE LAST BUILD

The big one is the recorder. The changelog has the full list, under
Unreleased. The ones you will notice:

- F11 records the slice you're on and Ctrl+F11 plays it back. Alt+F11
  records to a file on this computer and Ctrl+Alt+F11 plays that file.
  Playback starts and stops with a tape-deck click; recording keeps its
  beeps.
- Your radio's recordings show their length on Home, even after you
  reconnect.
- For checking your own audio, use the Audio Workshop's Audio Check
  with Listen method set to "Record and play back". It records your
  transmission on the radio and plays it back to you. It stops on its
  own at the recorder's two-minute limit.
- The reference recording stays armed between transmissions and keeps
  VOX off until you untick it.
- After the high reflected power alarm cuts a transmission, the radio
  won't key again for five seconds, unless you change your antenna,
  power, frequency or tuner.
- Holding Shift+Space locks or unlocks once, instead of keying the
  radio on and off.
- Ctrl+Shift+L takes you in and out of Logging mode, and leaving it
  tells you which tuning mode you're back in.
- Under NVDA, words like "can't" are spoken properly again.
- The meters come back after a reconnect.

KNOWN ISSUES

- Just after you turn VOX on, the radio can key itself for about a
  third of a second. It looks like the radio doing it, not the app; I'm
  still looking into it.
- Shift+Space doesn't lock the transmitter inside dialogs yet. Use
  Alt+Shift+Space there.
