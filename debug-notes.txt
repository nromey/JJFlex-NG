WHAT IS NEW AND WHERE TO FIND IT

Two things you reported are fixed in this build. Both were real, both were
ours, and neither was anything you did wrong.

VOX NOW WORKS

You were right and I was wrong about where to look. The radio's VOX detector
needs to hear audio BEFORE it can decide to key, and we were only sending
audio once transmit had already started. So while you were receiving, the
radio heard digital silence and nothing to trigger on. Turning the gain up
does not help, because no amount of gain turns silence into speech. That is
why your gain sweeps did nothing.

The microphone now streams the whole time PC audio is on, so the radio has
something to listen to. I tested five ways on my 8600 into a dummy load:
VOX off and idle stays unkeyed, VOX on with the mic live and nobody talking
stays unkeyed, VOX on and talking keys it, the test tone still stops dead
the instant you unkey, and with PC audio off nothing opens the microphone
at all.

One thing worth knowing about how we do it, since it is our choice and not
the radio's: we start the microphone stream whenever PC audio is on, rather
than only while you are transmitting. That is what gives VOX something to
hear. It costs about 9 KB a second and it means the stream is already there
when we add recording and playback later.

YOU SHOULD GET SLICES ON CONNECT AGAIN

This one is my regression and I am sorry about it. On 21 September I replaced
the code that sets up your station on connect, and the new version had a
safety gate in it waiting on a measurement nobody had taken. Until that
measurement existed the gate refused to create any slices, which is exactly
what you hit on 29 September. It had nothing to do with your ownership
setting or your profiles. The measurement is done and the gate is open.

ONE STEP YOU HAVE TO DO BY HAND. This bit matters, because it is what
unblocked me this morning and you will almost certainly need it too.

Connect, then open the Radio menu, then Profiles on This Radio. There are
three choices and only one of them loads your profiles:

  Use My Transmit Audio Here
  Load All My Profiles Here      <-- pick this one on your own radio
  Leave This Radio's Profiles Alone

If you are on Use My Transmit Audio Here, the app deliberately will not
touch your profiles, so you get slices at whatever frequency the radio
feels like, which is usually 14.100. Pick Load All My Profiles Here,
disconnect, reconnect, and your own frequencies should come back.

That menu is confusing and it is going away. It asks a standing question in
a place meant for actions, and it hides under a name that does not tell you
it controls what happens on connect. That is being redesigned, so do not
spend effort learning it.

ONE ODD MESSAGE YOU CAN IGNORE

When the profile load works, the diagnostic log still records the station as
not established. That is a third safety gate, waiting on its own measurement,
and all it means is that we cannot PROVE the radio finished loading, not that
anything failed. If your frequencies are right, it worked. You will not see
this unless you go looking in the log.
