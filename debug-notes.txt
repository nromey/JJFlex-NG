Evening build, August 6, 2026. Everything from build 517's sign-in
overhaul is here, plus an evening of radio selector improvements.

New since build 517:

1. Press Enter on a radio in the list to connect. The list has promised
   this forever; now it is true.
2. Start Remote automatically: Manage SmartLink Accounts has a new
   checkbox, "Start Remote automatically at startup." Check it and the
   radio selector begins looking for your remote radios the moment it
   opens. No more pressing Remote every session.
3. The Remote button becomes "Refresh Remote List" once your remote
   radios are listed. Same spot, same Alt+R. Pressing it reconnects to
   SmartLink and looks again, picking up radios that came online since
   you first asked. Radios that went offline drop out with a spoken
   notice.
4. Use Now: use an account for just this session without changing your
   default. New button in Manage SmartLink Accounts, Alt+U.
5. If every slice on the radio is busy, JJ Flexible now tells you who
   has them and backs out on its own instead of sitting at
   "Connecting" forever.
6. A radio on your local network that powers off now leaves the list
   about 20 seconds later, with a spoken notice.

Things to test:

1. Enter to connect from the radio list.
2. The auto-start checkbox: check it, restart the app, listen for
   "Starting remote radios for your account."
3. Refresh Remote List after your radios appear. It should be quick.
4. With the selector open, power a local radio off. It should announce
   "went offline" within about 20 seconds.

Known: item 6 (local radio leaving the list) landed at the end of the
evening and has not been verified live yet. First test tomorrow
morning.
