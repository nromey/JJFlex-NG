WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE LAST BUILD

Thanks for the Check your audio writeup. This build is a round of
fixes around it, most of them about the 6300 and about tune. The ones
you will notice:

- On a 6300, recording yourself at 0 watts used to play back nothing.
  Check your audio now runs at Low power there, from 1 watt, and puts
  your power back when the check ends. Test your microphone still runs
  at zero, because nothing goes on the air.
- The 6300 can't be set to 0 watts in the app any more: the arrows
  stop at 1, and a typed 0 says why. If the radio does read 0, a press
  that would key it tells you so instead of keying silently.
- Tune says what the radio did. "Tune on" waits for the radio to
  report it is transmitting, and a refused tune tells you why.
- Tune no longer stays on quietly. After 25 seconds you hear a tone and
  "Tune is still on", every 25 seconds. If you tune up a long time, you
  can stretch that to half an hour on the PTT page in Settings.
- While tune is on, recording is refused, and playing a recording says
  it won't go out on the air.
- With tune on, Escape in Check your audio turns tune off and leaves the
  window open. Ctrl+Shift+T works inside that window too.
- Ctrl+Enter in Check your audio starts a check on the first press
  after a take has played to its end.
- Save Station Setup to Radio is on the Radio menu now, right after
  Profiles on This Radio.
- After a disconnect, the window title says no radio connected instead
  of naming the slice you had.
- The connect tone plays out whole, and "1 watts" reads "1 watt".

KNOWN ISSUES

- Check your audio transmits on your transmit slice, which may not be
  the slice you are listening to, and it does not yet say which. Before
  a check, make sure the slice you listened on is the transmit slice.
- Just after you turn VOX on, the radio can key itself for about a
  third of a second. It looks like the radio doing it, not the app; I'm
  still looking into it.
- A hand mic or footswitch on the radio's own PTT jack keys the radio
  with the radio's microphone, so your computer microphone is silent
  on the air, and nothing tells you yet. A fix is planned.
- With VOX on, the Fix tool's transmit stage can say "passed" when it
  did not transmit at all.
- Shift+Space doesn't lock the transmitter inside dialogs yet. Use
  Alt+Shift+Space there.
