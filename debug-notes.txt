WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE LAST BUILD

The last build was November 2, so this one carries three weeks of
work. The full list is long; these are the ones you are most likely
to notice.

Please note: This build does not include any noise reduction or audio processing fixes or additions. i am finalizing the additions, testing various parts of the new systems we've added, and just making sure that all of it works. The changes you'll experience with this build are fixes and additions that are outside the pervue of any audio processing. I also plan on doing some other upgrades and efficiency saving measures. Stay tuned for a rediculous raft of additions to be released in a week or less.

Before You START

- JJ Flexible Radio Access now needs your callsign before it keys the radio, for
  push to talk, the transmit lock, tune, a send or a check. Enter
  yours under Radio, Operators.
- Your update choices (channel, checks on launch, skipped version)
  were never actually saved before. They are now, so please set them
  once more in Settings.

SMARTLINK

- SmartLink tries your forwarded ports and UPnP ports in order, and
  says when the connection method changes. A connection only counts
  as made once radio setup completes and audio or data arrives.
- A hole-punch port saved for the whole account by an older version
  is no longer used. Set a fixed port for one radio under Per-radio
  settings, or leave it blank and JJ Flexible picks one each time.
- Connecting to a radio on one of your other saved SmartLink accounts
  goes straight through that account, with no ten-second wait.
- Cancelling the SmartLink sign-in, or closing the browser sign-in
  page, now just says "Login cancelled", with no failure message
  after it. Escape closes the sign-in page.
- Radios on your own network sit at the top of the radio list.

CHECKING HOW YOU SOUND

- Play takes is a new window listing every take of your audio on
  this computer, newest first. Play, compare, add a note, save a
  copy or delete. Ctrl+J then Alt+T opens it, and Check your audio
  has a Play takes button.
- Check your audio's methods that make a take are now called Radio
  recording, Heard on the air, and Looped back.
- The reference recording now ends the transmission when it ends,
  and fits inside the radio's two-minute recorder.
- F11 warns before replacing a recording on the radio, and offers
  to back it up first.

TUNING, FILTERS AND THE RADIO

- On a radio with RF gain of just 0 and 20, like the 6300, Home has
  an RF preamp switch instead of a two-stop number field.
- With AGC off you can now set the gain: the AGC field becomes "AGC
  Off Gain".
- Filter widths say exactly what they are, and filter changes tell
  you the filter you actually got.
- Heads up: filter edge mode has new keys. Alt+Shift+Left Bracket
  grabs the low edge, Alt+Shift+Right Bracket the high edge. Two
  quick bracket presses no longer grab an edge.
- Heads up: Up and Down on the Slice fields now tune by your coarse
  step. Ctrl+Up and Down tune coarse, and Shift+Up and Down tune
  fine, from every field on Home's slice row.
- You can add and remove your own coarse and fine tuning steps in
  the list S opens.
- You can choose which modes Alt+M steps through, under Settings,
  Notifications, Mode Cycle.
- Ctrl+T sends a tune carrier when the tuner isn't on automatic, and
  the ATU switch no longer turns itself off after a tune.
- When the radio reports another station transmitting, push to talk
  and the transmit lock refuse and tell you why.

KNOWN ISSUES

- The noise reduction work in progress is not in this build yet.
- JJ Flexible does not yet enforce the US 60 meter power limits.
