WHAT IS NEW SINCE THE LAST BUILD

The big one is Recorded QSOs, the window for the recordings you make on
this computer. The changelog has the full list, under Unreleased. The
ones you will notice:

- Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F11 opens Recorded QSOs. Up and Down pick a recording,
  Enter plays and pauses it, S stops, and Left and Right (or Ctrl+Left
  and Ctrl+Right from anywhere in the window) step through it, down to
  a tenth of a second. [ and ] mark a trim, Save As writes it out as
  FLAC, MP3, Opus, WAV or M4A, and Delete and Prune send recordings to
  the Recycle Bin.
- While a recording plays back on your computer, the band drops behind
  it and comes back up when you pause or stop, as a smooth crossfade.
  Settings, Audio, "Radio audio during playback" sets how far, or mutes
  it.
- Dialogs play a rising pair of tones when they open and a falling pair
  when they close. Settings has a switch for them under Dialog and panel
  sounds.
- Settings is in proper groups now, so your screen reader names each
  section as you enter it, and F6 and Shift+F6 jump between sections.
- The Home slice row has Mode, Mute and Volume, and period and comma
  create and release a slice from any field on it. Typing a frequency,
  period is still the decimal point.
- Shift+S speaks your step sizes from any Home field in Modern tuning.
- A JJ key near miss tells you the key you pressed isn't assigned, and
  the JJ key lets go if you switch to another program.
- Manage Messages says when it is empty and has a Close button, and the
  Hotkey Editor lets Tab move on while it waits for a key.

KNOWN ISSUES

- Just after you turn VOX on, the radio can key itself for about a
  third of a second. It looks like the radio doing it, not the app; I'm
  still looking into it.
- Shift+Space doesn't lock the transmitter inside dialogs yet. Use
  Alt+Shift+Space there.
