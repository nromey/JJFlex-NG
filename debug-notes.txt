Hello Don — this one is mostly for you, and there are three specific things
only you can answer. Your radio has been back since Monday night, and this
build carries the fixes that were waiting on it.

WHAT I NEED YOU TO TRY

1. The tone that used to gallop.
   Arm the test tone and transmit for a good long while — a minute or more,
   longer than feels necessary. It should stay dead steady. It was wobbling
   before, and it is fixed and confirmed here at my bench, but never once over
   SmartLink at a real station, which is your setup and not mine.

2. Your keyboard shortcuts.
   Some command numbering changed under the hood a week ago, and any key map
   written before that would have loaded with keys attached to the wrong
   commands. This build repairs that automatically on first launch. Please just
   run through the shortcuts you actually use and tell me if ANY of them do
   something you did not expect. This is the one I most want to hear about,
   because a wrong key is worse than a missing one.

3. Push to talk, under JAWS.
   Hold Control plus Space and speak for ten or fifteen seconds. If the
   transmitter drops out and comes back several times a second while you are
   still holding the key, that is the bug I am chasing. It does not happen
   under NVDA, which is why I need your ears specifically. The build watches
   for it and writes to the log by itself — nothing changes in how it behaves,
   so if it is happening you should hear it.

ALSO NEW, AND WORTH A LOOK IF YOU HAVE TIME

Tools, then Fix, then Transmit problems. This is new: it walks your transmit
chain a stage at a time and tells you where it dies. Stages 0 and 1 do not key
the radio at all. Stage 2 onward will transmit, and it will not transmit
anything until you have told it what is on the antenna socket.

It is honest about what it cannot see, and it produces a report written to be
pasted into an email to Flex support. Have a poke at it if you are curious, but
it is not what this build is for and I am not asking you to test it yet.

The Audio Workshop also got rearranged today. Transmit audio used to be one
enormous page; it is now three shorter ones — This Computer, Transmit Settings,
and Hear Yourself. And the two meter pages are one page called Meters, with the
full meter list behind a button.

KNOWN AND NOT YET FIXED

- Nobody has pressed the buttons in the new Fix tool on a live radio yet.
  Automated checks prove the buttons reach the application, which is not the
  same as proving they do the right thing to your rig.
- If you have two microphones with the same name in Windows, the application
  can pick the wrong one. Uncommon, but it is real, and I know about it.

As always — anything that sounds wrong, sounds odd, or just annoys you, tell
me. Especially the small annoying things. Those are the ones that never get
reported and never get fixed.
