WHAT IS NEW AND WHERE TO FIND IT

JJ Flexible Fix. Open the Tools menu, then Fix, then Transmit problems.

It walks your transmit chain one stage at a time and tells you where it dies,
rather than leaving you to guess. Five stages, numbered 0 to 4, with a report
at the bottom you can copy straight into an email.

BEFORE YOU RUN IT

It asks what your antenna socket is connected to. Nothing transmits until you
answer that question, so if you are not sure, leave it unanswered — the checks
that transmit will wait, and everything else still runs.

Stages 0 and 1 do not key the radio at all. Stage 2 onward does, and each one
says so before you run it.
